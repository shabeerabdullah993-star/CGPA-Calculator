About this tool

This CGPA Calculator helps students work out their semester GPA and cumulative CGPA on a standard 4.0 scale. Add your courses with their credit hours and grades, and the tool totals everything automatically — no manual math, no spreadsheet.

How it works

Each course you add contributes quality points equal to its credit hours multiplied by its grade point. Your semester GPA is the total quality points divided by total credit hours for that semester. Your CGPA does the same calculation across every semester you've entered, combined.

Privacy

All calculations happen directly in your browser. Nothing you type — course names, grades, or credit hours — is sent anywhere or stored on any server. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything.
